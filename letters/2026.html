Dan Goergen President, Greater Peninsula Swimming Association

Greater Peninsula Swimming Association 2026 President's Letter Dan Goergen, President · August 2, 2026

Dear GPSA Swimmers and Families,

What an incredible 2026 season it has been for the Greater Peninsula Swimming Association! From the first dual meet in June through City Meet at the Hampton Virginia Aquaplex, it was another summer full of hard work, great racing, and community.

I want to start by offering a huge congratulations to our three undefeated division champions: the Windy Point Piranhas of the Red Division, the Running Man Manta Rays of the White Division, and the James River River Ratz of the Blue Division. Your perfect seasons were a testament to your hard work and team spirit.

This season we were excited to welcome the Village Green Patriots to GPSA. With Village Green on board, our league thrived with 18 teams and more than 1,350 athletes across the Virginia Peninsula.

We opened the summer with the J. Kyle Hurdle Swim Clinic, which helps get the season started and supports the scholarship fund that bears Kyle’s name. A warm thank you to Kyle’s sister for stopping by with donuts this year — it meant a lot to everyone there.

A major highlight was the second year of the Summer Splash Invitational at the Aquaplex, with about 280 swimmers on deck. There were nearly 290 time improvements, and 40 swimmers earned a combined 43 new City Meet qualifying times. For most it was a fun way to finish the season at a great facility; for others it was one more chance to qualify for City Meet. Congratulations to everyone who swam, and thank you to the volunteers who made the meet run.

Behind the scenes, the league also took a hard look at our City Meet qualifying times. With three seasons of results to compare, we could finally see event by event which standards were too hard, which were too easy, and where things were uneven across age groups. From that review, we adopted updated City Meet standards for 2026 and 2027 — so more swimmers have a fair shot at getting there.

On August 1, 512 swimmers from all 18 teams filled the Aquaplex for City Meet — up from 438 a year ago. The racing was outstanding, with more than 630 improved times across the day. Congratulations to the Windy Point Piranhas, 2026 City Meet team champions with 292 points. To every swimmer who made it there: making City Meet is something to be proud of.

Congratulations as well to our 2026 J. Kyle Hurdle Scholarship recipients — six graduating seniors:

Women

  1. 1st Taryn Wheeler Glendale
  2. 2nd Julia Hooks Riverdale
  3. 3rd Caitlin Wilson Warwick Yacht

Men

  1. 1st Reagan Wall Windy Point
  2. 2nd Tristan Bakian Wendwood
  3. 3rd Tony DeVries Poquoson

Well done to all six, and thank you to the families and coaches who supported them along the way.

Congratulations to Lena Shandor of the Running Man Manta Rays, recipient of the 2026 Kei Lamberson Outstanding Coach Award. Kei was known for joy, compassion, and connecting with every swimmer — the same qualities the Running Man families saw in Coach Shandor all season. Thank you for everything you give on deck.

A season like this is only possible through the tireless efforts of our entire community. Every swimmer across all 18 teams who swam for their team this summer made our league special. A special thank you goes to our GPSA Representatives, who spend countless hours behind the scenes planning meets, organizing the season with the league board, and often serving as the main coordinators for their own teams. To our Coaches, thank you for pouring yourselves into your swimmers. You not only teach the critical life skill of swimming but also foster a deep love for this great sport. To the Swimmers, we are in awe of your dedication — all those hours spent perfecting your strokes, building strength, and cheering on your teammates. And to our incredible GPSA Families, none of this would happen without you. Thank you for volunteering at every meet, driving to every practice, and being the best support system a swimmer could ask for. Together, you create a place where kids can love swimming, make friends, and learn what it means to be part of a team.

Thank you all for a great summer, and congratulations on a fantastic season!

Sincerely, Dan Goergen President, Greater Peninsula Swimming Association
